PROJECT DESCRIPTION (1122/1400 characters, including spaces and paragraph breaks)
I designed the Solates Squarespace website and wrote its content to help visitors choose between Pilates classes, sound healing and more personal support. The challenge was giving each offering enough explanation while keeping the next step easy to find.

I started with the content structure. Class descriptions explain the experience, level and suitability, so someone new to the studio has more to go on than a class name. Pricing, memberships and schedule links give that explanation a practical next step. Private sessions and training have separate routes for visitors looking for a different kind of support.

I used the founder's story and studio photography to introduce the person and place behind the practice. Testimonials add another perspective, while preparation and first-visit information help answer questions before booking. The sound-bowl collection has its own presentation within the same visual identity.

Warm beige, black serif headings and spacious layouts bring the pages together. The result is a website that explains the studio's range clearly and helps visitors find a suitable way to begin.
