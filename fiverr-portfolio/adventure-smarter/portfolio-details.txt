DESCRIPTION (1193/1400 characters, including spaces and paragraph breaks)
I designed and developed Adventure Smarter's Squarespace website to help visitors move from travel ideas to a planning conversation. The main challenge was explaining what a travel advisor handles, why the service is worth considering, and how to get started without making the site feel like another research task.

I organised the pages around those decisions. The homepage introduces the advisor and her specialisms, while the Services page separates the full planning package from additional support. Package details, a five-step planning process and FAQs make the scope easier to understand before an inquiry.

To build trust, I made room for the founder's story, client feedback, press mentions and sample itineraries. Those sections give visitors context for the service beyond destination photography. I also connected planning calls and inquiry options with clear buttons, and included email-guide sections for people who are still comparing their options.

The visual direction uses travel imagery, map details and a warm cream-and-blue palette to keep the brand personal. The result is a site that helps visitors explore the service, understand the process and find their next step.
