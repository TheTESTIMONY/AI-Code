PROJECT DESCRIPTION (1252/1,400 characters, including spaces and paragraph breaks)
I developed Interwise's Squarespace website around a practical challenge: helping visitors understand how investing and tax planning work together without getting lost in technical detail. The build needed to explain a wide range of services and give different client groups a clear way into the site.

I organised the pages around those needs. The Services page groups specialties such as tax, investment and retirement planning into expandable sections, so visitors can explore one topic at a time. Dedicated pages for entrepreneurs, executives, real estate investors and medical professionals connect the firm's support to the questions each audience is likely to have.

I also gave the team information a clear place in the experience, with professional profiles, biography links and credentials. The Insights section provides a separate home for educational articles and topic categories, keeping longer reading away from the main service journey.

To bring the journey together, I connected prominent meeting buttons to the external scheduling page and included a step-by-step introduction to working with the firm. The result is a Squarespace website that helps visitors explore the expertise, understand the services and take a clear next step.
