# HBCU Change: a short GoHighLevel walkthrough

Use one fictional **School A** record in the real HighLevel interface. Show the same school from start to finish rather than opening three unrelated tool menus. Keep the recording focused on the team's experience, with the setup editor shown only when it explains why something happens.

| Time | Show | Natural spoken point | What the founders learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | The fictional school record and its current stage | “I want to show what this could look like for one school, from its first inquiry to the point where we review its progress.” | This is a proposed HBCU Change process, not a general platform tour. |
| 0:20–1:00 | The board, the record, the responsible person and the next action | “Here we can see the school’s stage, who is handling it and what is still needed. If the workflow or reporting is not configured yet, say **“This is the configuration I’m proposing”** and show the actual draft without implying messages have been sent or results have been measured. Show working test behaviour where it is available. Use no invented success rates, saved hours or fundraising results.

Before recording, use a dedicated fictional record and test contact, close unrelated conversations/tabs, and avoid showing real contacts, phone numbers, emails, credentials or private conversations. If reporting has no test data, explain the fields and questions without inserting fabricated performance numbers. Label any sample figures clearly as test activity.

If the video will appear on the page, provide captions and a short text outline or transcript. Use a real thumbnail from the recording. Put the recording after the recommendation and before or alongside the detailed pipeline explanation. Do not publish a playable-looking placeholder while the video is missing.
