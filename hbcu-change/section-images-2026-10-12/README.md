## What these images are

The uploaded **imagegen-frontend-web** skill calls for one separate horizontal frontend design reference per section. These images therefore contain section layout, typography, imagery and illustrative controls. They are design references, not a replacement of the functioning HTML with screenshots, and not isolated text-free photo assets. The original headline and natural first-person proposal language guide the designs. The earlier HTML remains authoritative for the full explanation, exact production wording, scope qualifications and working controls. Generated incidental navigation and demonstration copy is illustrative.

All campus scenes, people and workspace photographs in this set are generated and fictional. They do not document an actual school, person, partnership, testimonial or finished system. School A and School B remain examples. Prices come from the supplied proposal and are not independently verified.

## Art direction

**Clean:** light green-white surfaces, navy text, restrained green accents and Outfit-like geometric type. Daylight campus scenes, workspace details and a woven green fabric crop support a readable, personal proposal. The narrative follows the journey between schools and the team. The opening has broad typography, while the school-identity section uses an inverted image-led composition. Thin lines organize the rollout and FAQs. Selected visual components: off-grid editorial composition, layered image crops, horizontal accordion slices and vertical rhythm lines. Intended motion cues: smooth accordion expansion and pinned narrative sections; the PNGs themselves are static.

**Studio:** deep navy surfaces, pale text, soft mint accents and Satoshi-like refined type. Dusk campus scenes and darker workspaces give the same proposal a more immersive presentation. The narrative emphasizes a workspace for coordinating partnerships. Product panels appear where they clarify the workflow or school identity, with quieter sections between them. Selected visual components: off-grid editorial composition, purposeful panel stacks, horizontal accordion slices and vertical rhythm lines. Intended motion cues: scrubbing text and cinematic fade-through; the PNGs themselves are static.

Both sets vary their composition through wide headings, inverted splits, image-as-canvas sections, contextual crops and quieter typographic sections. The main actions use a filled button; secondary actions use underlined links. The school-identity section carries a tactile detail in each direction: woven material in Clean and a mint type-specimen sheet in Studio. Palette values are direction targets, not a claim of pixel-exact generated colours or licensed font reproduction.

## Review performed

Every generated image was inspected for hierarchy, legibility, relevant imagery, horizontal framing and consistency with its design direction. Both openings retain the original long headline. Cost references show $95, $97 and the optional +$10 Notion amount, with usage and implementation qualifications. App replacement and app-data limitations remain visible in the relevant references. The Clean workflow initially generated a vertical accordion; it was regenerated into the intended side-by-side arrangement. Only the corrected version is included.

All 21 final PNGs were decoded and verified, their dimensions and hashes recorded in manifest.json, and the gallery’s local destinations and filters checked. ZIP integrity was checked. Gallery browser rendering was not tested in this environment. These are visual directions; production accessibility, selectable text, responsive reflow and real interactions must be implemented in HTML, not inferred from the raster images. Small generated labels and photographic detail should receive a final editorial check when transferring a reference into code.
