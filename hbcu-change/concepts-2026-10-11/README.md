# HBCU Change — three landing-page concepts

[Download the complete concept set](https://github.com/TheTESTIMONY/AI-Code/raw/refs/heads/main/hbcu-change/concepts-2026-10-11/hbcu-change-concepts.zip)

Unzip the package and open **index.html** in your browser to compare the three complete designs, switch between desktop and mobile widths, view the original proposal, and read the design review.

| Concept | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **01 / Clear path** | Bright, structured, and recommended for the proposal. |
| **02 / In the flow** | Dark, immersive, and focused on the proposed workflow. |
| **03 / Shared future** | Warm, editorial, and focused on the school community. |

All three designs retain the original opening and the natural first-person proposal voice. They explain what is being proposed, why it would help, and what the author would set up; repeated recap sections have been removed.

Each concept is also a standalone HTML file with embedded imagery and fonts. Interactions include school identity switching, a four-step walkthrough with pause, a cost toggle, FAQs, and a discussion agenda.

Read the [full design comparison](design-review.md) for the evaluation of Calendly, CleanShot, and the supplied proposal.

The records and imagery are conceptual. Prices are carried over from the supplied proposal, not independently verified. No platform connection or message sending is implemented.

Validation: JavaScript syntax, HTML structure and link targets, simulated DOM interactions, reduced-motion controls, and ZIP integrity passed. Browser visual QA and real-device testing remain outstanding.
