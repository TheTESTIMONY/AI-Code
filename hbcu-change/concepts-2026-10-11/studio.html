Before we move forward.

Does this change our fundraising apps?

No. The apps remain the fundraising product. This proposal covers websites, partnerships and campaign operations; connecting app data is a separate scope.

Do we need a new platform to track app-store clicks?

No. That tracking can also be added to Squarespace. The wider reason for this proposal is the combination of school websites, follow-ups, booking and campaign tools.

Would everything happen automatically?

No. Only agreed routine steps would be configured and tested. The team would continue to handle conversations, review content, manage exceptions and approve launches.

What would we confirm before committing?

Current pricing, included features, school permissions, expected usage costs, the migration plan, implementation scope and fee. We would also decide whether to retain Notion.
