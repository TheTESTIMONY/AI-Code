Every school, its own identity

A website that
feels like their own.

Each school has its own identity. Its website should reflect that through its fonts, colours, imagery, and overall presentation.

Our current arrangement puts school pages inside HBCU Change’s shared styling. Adapting them has required custom code and separately designed graphics, with compromises around typography.

Dedicated native HighLevel websites would give each school its own design settings, including custom fonts, while keeping the sites managed in one platform.

A more faithful school brand.
Fewer workarounds for the team.

Reusable structures are already part of our work. This change adds independent design control; bespoke code and maintenance may still be needed.

A few practical questions

Let’s keep
the details clear.

The proposal gives us a direction.
These decisions shape the final scope.

Does this replace the fundraising apps?

No. The apps remain HBCU Change’s fundraising product. The proposed websites promote them, support each school’s identity, and guide supporters to Apple App Store and Google Play links.

Can each school keep its own identity?

Yes. The proposed native sites have separate design settings for typography, colours, layouts, and content. HighLevel supports custom fonts. Custom code may still be needed for bespoke requirements.

Will Notion definitely be replaced?

That is still a decision to make. We need to understand what Notion handles today. If it stays, the proposed monthly base is $107: HighLevel at $97 plus Notion at $10.

Will all the reporting live in one dashboard?

Not automatically. Operational records and available native marketing reports can support the initial view. GA4 may remain separate, and app or donation outcomes need a confirmed export or integration. A combined overview may need supplementary reporting.

What stays with the team?

Relationship decisions, asset review, content approval, and launch approval stay human. Configured automation supports routine acknowledgements, reminders, and relevant follow-up. The founders oversee and decide; team members carry out the work.

Do schools need their own account or CRM access?

The working proposal uses one centrally managed HBCU Change subaccount. No need for school staff CRM access has been established. School tags, fields, and folders are organisational tools. Account separation should be revisited if privacy, access, or ownership requirements call for it.
